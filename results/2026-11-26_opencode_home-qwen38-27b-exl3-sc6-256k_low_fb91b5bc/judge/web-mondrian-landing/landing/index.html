Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Oil on canvas · Kunsthaus Zurich

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow

The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. This canvas is from 1930, the mature period of his abstract work.

The movement

The work belongs to De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Its principles — Neoplasticism — allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

red · blue · yellow · white · grey · black

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

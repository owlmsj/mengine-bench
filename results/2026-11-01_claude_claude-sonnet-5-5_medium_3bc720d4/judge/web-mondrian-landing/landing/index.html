The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872-1944), Dutch painter, co-founder of De Stijl. Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

Medium
Oil on canvas
Collection
Kunsthaus Zurich

The year

1930

The movement

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Its principles

Neoplasticism reduces painting to essentials:

  • Only horizontal and vertical lines
  • The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow
  • The non-colours: white, grey and black

Why it matters

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground. This asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

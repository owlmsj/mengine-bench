01 · The movement

De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its painting programme, Neoplasticism, reduced the picture to a strict set of rules:

Lines Only horizontal and vertical lines.
Primary colours Red, blue and yellow.
Non-colours White, grey and black.
02 · The picture

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

03 · Why it matters

A reference for modern design

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

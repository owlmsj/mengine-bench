Piet Mondrian · 1930 · De Stijl

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

The picture

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

At a glance

Painter
Piet Mondrian (1872-1944), Dutch painter
Year
1930
Medium
Oil on canvas
Collection
Kunsthaus Zurich
Also listed as
Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

Neoplasticism

Only horizontal and vertical lines. The three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

The movement

De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

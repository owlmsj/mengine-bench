01 The language

Less to see.
More to notice.

Horizontal and vertical lines. Three primary colours. White, grey and black. These are the principles of Neoplasticism, the visual vocabulary behind this composition.

A vocabulary of essentials

01

Colour, reduced. The primary colours red, blue and yellow, alongside the non-colours white, grey and black.

02

Lines, simplified. Only horizontal and vertical lines; thick black divisions on a white ground.

03

A shared movement. De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

02 The legacy

A few blocks.
A lasting reference.

The painting’s asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

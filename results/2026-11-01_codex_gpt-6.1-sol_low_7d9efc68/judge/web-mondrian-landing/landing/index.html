01 / The painter & the painting

Piet Mondrian

1872–1944 · Dutch painter

In Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, Mondrian brings a whole visual world into a handful of rectangles. A large red square anchors the upper right; small blue and yellow shapes punctuate the lower corners. Thick black lines hold the white ground in balance.

02 / The movement

Less form.
More possibility.

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian, De Stijl sought a reduced visual language. Its principles of Neoplasticism distilled composition into horizontal and vertical lines, primary colours, and non-colours.

┼

Essential geometry

Red, blue and yellow. A focused palette.

Non-colours

White, grey and black create structure and space.

03 / Why it matters

Balance without
symmetry.

The blocks differ in size, colour and position, yet the composition holds together. This asymmetric balance became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture: a way to make a few simple elements feel complete.

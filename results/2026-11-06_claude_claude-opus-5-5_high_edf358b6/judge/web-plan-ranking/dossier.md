**Assumptions:** the Real launch date is 1994-07-01, when the currency was introduced (the URV phase began in March 1994). The Collor II day is 1991-01-31. Whole months are counted by calendar day.

**Reading:** only the Real both cut inflation (by 99%) and lasted. Collor I scores second only on the ratio: it froze savings and inflation came back within a year. Cruzado, Bresser and Verao each ended with inflation higher than before, so their drop score is 0.

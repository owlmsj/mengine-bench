De Stijl · 1930

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Piet Mondrian (1872-1944), Dutch painter

Painter
Piet Mondrian
Year
1930
Movement
De Stijl
Medium
Oil on canvas

The picture

A few blocks, in balance

A large red square sits at the top right. A small blue square holds the bottom left, and a small yellow rectangle the bottom right. Thick black lines divide them on a white ground.

The painting is also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow. It is in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

The movement

De Stijl

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

The rules

Neoplasticism

Only horizontal and vertical lines.

  • Red
  • Blue
  • Yellow
  • and the non-colours white, grey and black

Why it matters

Asymmetric balance

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

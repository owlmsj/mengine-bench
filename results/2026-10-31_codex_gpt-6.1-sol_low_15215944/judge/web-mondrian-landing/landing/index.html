04 / THE PRINCIPLES

Less, with purpose.

Founded by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian in 1917, De Stijl gave form to a radical simplicity. Neoplasticism uses only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Straight lines. Primary colours. Asymmetric balance.

05 / THE LASTING IMPACT

A new language
for modern life.

A large red square. Small blue and yellow accents. White space held together by thick black lines. This asymmetric balance became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture, showing how a few elements can shape an entire visual language.

A composition of contrastsBalance ≠ symmetry.

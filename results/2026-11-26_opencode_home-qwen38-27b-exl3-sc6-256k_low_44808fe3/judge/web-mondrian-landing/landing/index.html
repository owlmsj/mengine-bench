The painter

Piet Mondrian

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. This work, Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow (also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow), dates from 1930 and is painted in oil on canvas. It is held in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

The movement

De Stijl

The painting belongs to De Stijl, a movement founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, known as Neoplasticism, allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Lines

Only horizontal and vertical lines divide the picture plane.

Primary colours

Only red, blue and yellow appear as colour.

Non-colours

White, grey and black complete the palette.

Why it matters

Its legacy

The work’s asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

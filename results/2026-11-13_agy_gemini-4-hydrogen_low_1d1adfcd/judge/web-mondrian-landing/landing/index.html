Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow, this 1930 painting by Dutch painter Piet Mondrian distills visual expression to horizontal and vertical lines, primary colours, and non-colours.

Painter
Piet Mondrian (1872–1944), Dutch painter
Year & Medium
1930 · Oil on canvas
Movement
De Stijl (founded 1917)
Collection
Kunsthaus Zurich
De Stijl & Neoplasticism

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian, advancing the principles of Neoplasticism.

01 / Structure

Horizontal & Vertical Lines

Only horizontal and vertical lines are used, dividing the white ground with thick black lines.

02 / Primary Colours

Red, Blue & Yellow

Limited to the three primary colours: a large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left, and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right.

03 / Non-Colours

White, Grey & Black

Paired with the non-colours white, grey, and black to establish clarity and visual equilibrium.

Why It Matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

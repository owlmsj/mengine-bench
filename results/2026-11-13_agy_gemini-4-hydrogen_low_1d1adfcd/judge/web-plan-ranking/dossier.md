## Key Takeaways & Assumptions

- **Inflation metric:** Annual accumulated IPCA (IBGE) from December to December, sourced from [Ipeadata](https://www.ipeadata.gov.br/ExibeSerie.aspx?serid=38391) and [IBGE](https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/economicas/precos-e-custos/9256-indice-nacional-de-precos-ao-consumidor-amplo.html).
- **Plano Real launch date:** Dated to **1994-02-27** (MP 434, introduction of the URV) per [Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Real), rather than 1994-07-01 (currency conversion via MP 542, [Senado Federal](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2024/07/01/plano-real-completa-30-anos)). Using 1994-07-01 would extend Collor II's duration from 36 to 41 months without altering the top 3 ranking.
- **Whole calendar months:** Computed as complete calendar months between launch dates; Real is capped at 60 months per `RUBRIC.md`.

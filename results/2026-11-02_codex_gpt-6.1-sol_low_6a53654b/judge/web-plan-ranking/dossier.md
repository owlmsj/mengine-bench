# Brazil's stabilization plans: editor's dossier

**Finding.** Real wins the supplied test, followed by Collor I and Collor II. This is a ranking of the specified inflation and replacement-interval outcomes, not a verdict that every promise was fulfilled.

**Method and assumptions.** Use IBGE's published annual calendar-year IPCA, not INPC, IPC or monthly inflation. Each inflation cell links to the historical annual table (printed page eighteen). Before and after mean the calendar years immediately preceding and following the launch year. Use announcement dates; Real uses currency introduction, consistent with the rubric's launch year, although preparation began earlier ([BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/controleinflacao/planoreal?modalAberto=30anosreal_noticia1)). Store Verão as `Verao` to match the rubric.

“Whole months” means completed calendar-month anniversaries: subtract an unfinished final month. Follow only the successor chain in [RUBRIC.md](./RUBRIC.md), even where other measures intervened. Real's duration is the rubric's imposed cap, not an observed ending. Scores are calculated using the rubric, without intermediate rounding.

**Reading the ranking.** Real combines a near-total proportional inflation decline with maximum durability credit. Collor I earns substantial decline credit, but its after-year already includes Collor II. Collor II earns partial decline credit and benefits from the long gap until Real; its after-year still records extremely high inflation. Cruzado, Bresser and Verão receive no decline credit because their selected after-year exceeds their before-year (linked IBGE figures above). Bresser outranks Cruzado solely on the interval until its successor.

**Editorial caution.** Replacement intervals do not measure how long prices stayed stable. Annual comparisons mix predecessor and successor policies and cannot isolate causation, welfare costs or temporary improvements. A defensible headline is “Real wins the specified inflation-and-duration test.” Calling the runners-up durable stabilization successes would overstate this evidence.

**Audit trail.** All inflation observations come from the same IBGE annual table. Launch dates come from the linked historical or contemporary sources. The JSON keeps unrounded scores, every plan and its source URLs; the table rounds only for display. Derived months and scores cite their source inputs, with their calculation governed by the local rubric.

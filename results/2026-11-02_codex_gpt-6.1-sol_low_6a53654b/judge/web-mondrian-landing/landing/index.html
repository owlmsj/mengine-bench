01 / THE STORY

Few elements. Lasting influence.

The work’s asymmetric balance of primary-color blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

A movement: De Stijl

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

A principle: Neoplasticism

Only horizontal and vertical lines; red, blue and yellow; and the non-colors white, grey and black.

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Piet Mondrian, 1930 Oil on canvas · Kunsthaus Zurich

The Painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. Together with Theo van Doesburg he founded De Stijl in the Netherlands in 1917, the movement behind Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, painted in 1930.

The Movement: De Stijl

De Stijl followed the principles of Neoplasticism, which reduced painting to a strict visual grammar. Mondrian's canvas is that grammar made visible:

Lines

Only horizontal and vertical lines.

Colours

The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.

Non-colours

White, grey and black hold the composition together.

The Picture

On a white ground, thick black lines divide the canvas into unequal fields:

Large red square

Top right.

Small blue square

Bottom left.

Small yellow rectangle

Bottom right.

Thick black lines

Divide the white ground.

Why It Matters

The painting's asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

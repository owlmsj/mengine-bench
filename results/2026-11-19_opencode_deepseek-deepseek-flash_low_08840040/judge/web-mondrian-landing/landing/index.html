02The work

A few lines, three colours, one balance.

The picture is built from almost nothing: a large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

03The movement

De Stijl — founded in the Netherlands in 1917.

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its programme for painting is called Neoplasticism, and it is a set of exclusions.

  1. 01Only horizontal and vertical lines.
  2. 02Only the three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.
  3. 03Only the non-colours: white, grey and black.

04Why it matters

A few blocks that became a reference.

The painting's asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture — unequal parts, held by a grid, brought into balance.

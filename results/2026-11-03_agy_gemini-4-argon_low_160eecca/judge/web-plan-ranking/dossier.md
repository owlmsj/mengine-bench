# Dossier: Which of Brazil's Anti-Inflation Plans Delivered?

**Bottom Line for the Editor:** Only one stabilization program—the **Plano Real (1994)**—delivered both immediate disinflation and enduring monetary stability (`score = 0.9937` [RUBRIC.md](<WORKSPACE>/RUBRIC.md)). Although **Collor I (1990)** and **Collor II (1991)** rank second (`0.5823`) and third (`0.4217`) under the strict Year $Y-1$ vs. Year $Y+1$ calendar-year rubric, their scores reflect mechanical base effects and the 41-month policy hiatus following Zélia Cardoso de Mello's departure rather than genuine stabilization. The 1980s heterodox price freezes (**Bresser**, **Cruzado**, **Verão**) all failed the inflation-reduction test completely (`score_drop = 0.0000`), leaving inflation higher in $Y+1$ than in $Y-1$.

## 2. Analytical Narrative: Why the Top 3 Scored as They Did

### #1. Plano Real (`1994-07-01`) — Score: `0.9937`
- **Why it won:** Unlike its predecessors, the Plano Real avoided surprise price freezes and asset confiscations. Instead, it sequenced stabilization in three transparent steps: (1) fiscal adjustment (PAI / Fundo Social de Emergência), (2) coordinated de-indexation via a voluntary super-index, the **URV** (created `1994-02-27` by MP 434 ([Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Real))), which aligned relative prices in real time, and (3) monetary reform on `1994-07-01` under MP 542 ([Senado MP 542](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/553717)).
- **The numbers:** Annual IPCA fell from `2477.15%` in 1993 to `916.46%` in the transition year 1994 and `22.41%` in 1995 ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))—a `99.10%` relative reduction (`score_drop = 0.9910`) coupled with maximum durability (`60` months, `score_durability = 1.0000`).

### #2. Plano Collor I (`1990-03-16`) — Score: `0.5823`
- **Why it ranks #2 on the rubric (and the editorial caveat):** Launched one day after Fernando Collor's inauguration (`1990-03-16`, implementing MP 168 of `1990-03-15`, later Law 8.024 of `1990-04-12` ([Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Collor), [LexML Lei 8.024](https://www.lexml.gov.br/urn/urn:lex:br:federal:lei:1990-04-12;8024))), Collor I froze roughly 80% of private financial assets (savings and overnight accounts above NCz$ 50,000). Because 1989 ended in hyperinflation (`1972.91%` ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))), even a failed plan that required replacement in just `10` months (`1991-01-31` ([G1](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/entenda-os-planos-economicos-bresser-verao-collor-1-e-collor-2-e-as-perdas-na-poupanca.ghtml))) appears to show a `76.04%` inflation drop when comparing 1989 (`1972.91%`) to 1991 (`472.70%` ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))).
- **Reality check for readers:** At `472.70%` annual inflation in 1991 ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512)), prices were still quintupling every year, and the liquidity freeze triggered a deep recession.

### #3. Plano Collor II (`1991-01-31`) — Score: `0.4217`
- **Why it ranks #3:** Announced on `1991-01-31` via MP 294 ([G1](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/entenda-os-planos-economicos-bresser-verao-collor-1-e-collor-2-e-as-perdas-na-poupanca.ghtml), [Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Collor)), Collor II attempted another price freeze and replaced overnight indexation with the Taxa Referencial (TR). It scores `0.4217` for two mechanical reasons:
  1. **Base effect:** 1990 IPCA (`1620.97%` ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))) still carried the early-1990 hyperinflation spike, whereas 1992 IPCA (`1119.10%` ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))) was slightly lower (`score_drop = 0.3096`), even though inflation had actually *accelerated* from `472.70%` in 1991 to `1119.10%` in 1992 and `2477.15%` in 1993 ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512)).
  2. **Shock fatigue ("durability"):** After Collor II failed in May 1991, Minister Marcílio Marques Moreira adopted orthodox high interest rates without launching a named heterodox shock, and President Collor's 1992 impeachment delayed the next named plan (Real) for `41` months (`score_durability = 0.6833`).

### #4–#6. The 1980s Heterodox Freezes: Bresser (`0.0950`), Cruzado (`0.0750`), Verão (`0.0700`)
- **Cruzado (`1986-02-28`, DL 2.283/2.284 ([Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Cruzado), [LexML DL 2.283](https://www.lexml.gov.br/urn/urn:lex:br:federal:decreto.lei:1986-02-27;2283))):** Compressed 1986 IPCA to `79.66%` (from `242.23%` in 1985 ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))) via a general price freeze alongside expansionary fiscal/monetary policy, causing severe shortages and repressed inflation that exploded to `363.41%` in 1987 ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512)) (`score_drop = 0`).
- **Bresser (`1987-06-12`, DL 2.335 ([Migalhas DL 2.335](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/62420/ha-21-anos-o-decreto-lei-n--2-335-instituiu-o-plano-bresser))) & Verão (`1989-01-15`, MP 32 / Law 7.730 ([SciELO](https://www.scielo.br/j/rep/a/qSCVFmgyqNf44Xhdn7M3Mcz/?lang=pt), [LexML Lei 7.730](https://www.lexml.gov.br/urn/urn:lex:br:federal:lei:1989-01-31;7730))):** Each successive freeze lasted fewer weeks in practice as economic agents preemptively marked up prices. Both saw $Y+1$ inflation surge far above $Y-1$ (`980.21%` vs. `79.66%` for Bresser; `1620.97%` vs. `980.21%` for Verão ([IBGE SIDRA API](https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1737/n1/all/v/69/p/198512,198612,198712,198812,198912,199012,199112,199212,199312,199412,199512))), earning points solely from the months elapsed before the next emergency package.

## 3. Methodological Assumptions & Sensitivity Check

Where [RUBRIC.md](<WORKSPACE>/RUBRIC.md) leaves operational details open, we state and test our choices:
1. **Official Launch Dates:** We use the effective launch date of each stabilization package/monetary reform: Cruzado `1986-02-28` (effective date after evening announcement of DL 2.283 on Feb 27 ([Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Cruzado), [LexML DL 2.283](https://www.lexml.gov.br/urn/urn:lex:br:federal:decreto.lei:1986-02-27;2283))), Bresser `1987-06-12` ([Migalhas DL 2.335](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/62420/ha-21-anos-o-decreto-lei-n--2-335-instituiu-o-plano-bresser)), Verão `1989-01-15` ([SciELO](https://www.scielo.br/j/rep/a/qSCVFmgyqNf44Xhdn7M3Mcz/?lang=pt)), Collor I `1990-03-16` ([Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Collor)), Collor II `1991-01-31` ([G1](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/entenda-os-planos-economicos-bresser-verao-collor-1-e-collor-2-e-as-perdas-na-poupanca.ghtml)), and Real `1994-07-01` (currency launch under MP 542 ([Wikipedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plano_Real), [Senado MP 542](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/553717))).
   - *Sensitivity:* Using the URV decree date (`1994-02-27`) for Plano Real changes Collor II's `months_lasted` from `41` to `36` (Collor II score `0.3967` vs. `0.4217`), and using `1986-02-27` vs `1986-02-28` or `1990-03-15` vs `1990-03-16` leaves `months_lasted` (`15` and `10`) identical. **Under every date convention, the ranking of all 6 plans (`Real > Collor I > Collor II > Bresser > Cruzado > Verao`) is 100% invariant.**
2. **Whole Calendar Months (`months_lasted`):** Calculated as full elapsed calendar months between launch dates `(Y2 - Y1)*12 + (M2 - M1) - (1 if D2 < D1 else 0)`.
3. **Rounding:** Scores in [result.json](<WORKSPACE>/result.json) are rounded to 4 decimal places; IPCA values keep IBGE's exact 2 decimal places.

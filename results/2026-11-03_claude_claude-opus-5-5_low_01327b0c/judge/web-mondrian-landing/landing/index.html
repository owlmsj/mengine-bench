The movement: De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, called Neoplasticism, allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

The rules

  • Horizontal and vertical lines only
  • Red, blue, yellow
  • White, grey, black

Also known as

Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow

The picture

A large red square sits at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground. The opening of this page borrows that arrangement.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

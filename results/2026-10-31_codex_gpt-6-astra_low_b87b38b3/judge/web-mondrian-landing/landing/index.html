De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

An art reduced to its essentials. De Stijl’s principles of Neoplasticism give line, colour and space a precise vocabulary.

05 / Why it matters

Balance doesn’t
need symmetry.

A large red square anchors the top right. A small blue square and a yellow rectangle punctuate the lower corners. Thick black lines divide a white ground, holding these unequal parts in balance.

This asymmetric arrangement of just a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

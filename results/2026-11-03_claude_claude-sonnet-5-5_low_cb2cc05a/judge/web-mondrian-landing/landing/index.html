Piet Mondrian · 1930 · De Stijl

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

A large red square, two small blocks of colour, thick black lines on white. Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

1930 Oil on canvas

The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944), Dutch painter.

The movement

De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Where it hangs

Kunsthaus Zurich.

Neoplasticism: the rules

Only horizontal and vertical lines. The three primary colours red, blue and yellow. The non-colours white, grey and black.

  • Horizontal + vertical
  • Red
  • Blue
  • Yellow
  • White, grey, black

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

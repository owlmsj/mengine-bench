The painter and the movement

Piet Mondrian

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. In 1930 he painted Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow in oil on canvas. The work is in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

De Stijl

Mondrian worked within De Stijl, a movement founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Neoplasticism: the rules of this language

  • Only horizontal and vertical lines.
  • The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.
  • The non-colours: white, grey and black.

The picture follows those rules exactly: a large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

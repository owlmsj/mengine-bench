Piet Mondrian · 1872–1944 · Dutch painter

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

1930 — oil on canvas. A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

The picture

Asymmetric balance: three primary-colour blocks, thick black lines, white ground.

The movement

De Stijl

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles — Neoplasticism — allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Neoplasticism

only primaries

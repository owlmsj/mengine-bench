01 / THE LANGUAGE

Less, by design.

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Its Neoplasticism principles use only horizontal and vertical lines, the primary colors red, blue and yellow, and the non-colors white, grey and black.

02 / THE SIGNIFICANCE

A balance that
reached beyond art.

A large red square occupies the top right. A small blue square anchors the bottom left; a small yellow rectangle sits at the bottom right. Thick black lines divide the white ground.

This asymmetric balance of a few primary-color blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

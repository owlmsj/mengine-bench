The picture

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground. Oil on canvas, 1930, now in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

Movement — De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Principles — Neoplasticism

Only horizontal and vertical lines; the three primary colours red, blue and yellow; and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Why it matters

The pictureWhat is on the canvas

Three colours, set in balance.

On a white ground, thick black lines divide the canvas into a few plain areas. A large red square sits at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left, and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right.

The movementDe Stijl · Neoplasticism

A strict visual vocabulary.

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, known as Neoplasticism, allow only horizontal and vertical lines; the three primary colours red, blue and yellow; and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Horizontal & vertical

Lines meet at right angles — no diagonals.

Three primary colours

Red, blue and yellow.

Non-colours

White, grey and black.

Why it mattersThe work's reach

Few blocks. Lasting influence.

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

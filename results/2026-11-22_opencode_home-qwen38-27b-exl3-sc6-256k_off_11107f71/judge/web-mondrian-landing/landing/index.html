Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Piet Mondrian · 1930 · oil on canvas
Kunsthaus Zurich

The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. This work, also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow, dates from 1930 and is an oil on canvas.

The picture places a large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

The movement

De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Its principles, called Neoplasticism: only horizontal and vertical lines; the three primary colours red, blue and yellow; and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

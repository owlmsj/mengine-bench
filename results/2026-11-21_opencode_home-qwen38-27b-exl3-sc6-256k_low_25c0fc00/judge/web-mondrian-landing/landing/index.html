The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. This work, Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow — also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow — was painted in 1930 in oil on canvas and is held in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

The movement

Mondrian worked within De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles of Neoplasticism reduce painting to a strict visual grammar:

Lines

Only horizontal and vertical lines.

Primary colours

Red, blue and yellow.

Non-colours

White, grey and black.

The picture

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

02 / The movement

De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

The movement's principles, known as Neoplasticism, hold to:

  • Only horizontal and vertical lines.
  • Red, blue and yellow: the three primary colours.
  • White, grey and black: the non-colours.

Why it matters

A few blocks, an asymmetric balance.

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

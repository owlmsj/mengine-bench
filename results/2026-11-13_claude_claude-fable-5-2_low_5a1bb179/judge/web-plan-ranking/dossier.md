## Assumptions
- Real launch = currency introduction, 1 Jul 1994 (not the URV, Feb 1994); Real months fixed at 60 by rubric.
- Whole months = completed calendar months between launch dates.
- Cruzado II, the 1986 follow-up, is not in the rubric and is ignored.

## Reading
- Only Real, Collor I and Collor II score on inflation drop; Cruzado, Bresser and Verao saw higher IPCA in Y+1 than Y-1, so they score on durability only.
- Collor I outranks Collor II on the rubric although 1991 IPCA was still about 473%: the rubric rewards relative drop, not price stability. Worth saying in the piece.

The painter

Piet Mondrian

Piet Mondrian (1872-1944) was a Dutch painter. He painted this work in 1930, in oil on canvas. It is also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow, and it is in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

The movement

De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles are known as Neoplasticism.

The principles

Neoplasticism

  • Only horizontal and vertical lines.
  • The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.
  • The non-colours: white, grey and black.

The picture

Red, blue and yellow on white

A large red square at the top right. A small blue square at the bottom left. A small yellow rectangle at the bottom right. Thick black lines divide them on a white ground.

Why it matters

A reference for modern design

The asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

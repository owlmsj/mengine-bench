The movement and its principles

De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its programme, Neoplasticism, strips painting down to a short list of rules.

Fewer lines. Fewer colours. More balance.

Rule 1

Straight lines only

Only horizontal and vertical lines. No diagonals, no curves.

Rule 2

Three primaries

The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.

Rule 3

Three non-colours

White, grey and black.

The picture

Look closely

A large red square sits at the top right. A small blue square is at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right. Thick black lines divide them on a white ground.

The diagram is a simplified sketch, not a reproduction of the painting.

Why it matters

A reference

An asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks.

That composition became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

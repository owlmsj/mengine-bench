01

The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. By 1930 he had spent years reducing painting to its most essential elements: straight lines and primary colour.

1872–1944

Born and worked across the twentieth century; this canvas is dated 1930.

Dutch painter

The work is listed as Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, also as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

02

The movement: De Stijl

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, called Neoplasticism, allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours — red, blue and yellow — and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Red

Primary colour, used as a large square at the top right of the picture.

Blue

White

Grey

Black

Non-colour; the thick dividing lines.

03

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

Asymmetric balance

A few primary-colour blocks, unevenly placed, in equilibrium.

Legacy

A reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

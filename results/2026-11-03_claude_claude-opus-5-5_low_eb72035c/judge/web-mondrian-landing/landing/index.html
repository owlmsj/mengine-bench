Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

The painter

Piet Mondrian

Dutch painter, 1872-1944. In 1917 he founded the De Stijl movement in the Netherlands together with Theo van Doesburg.

The rules

Neoplasticism

Only horizontal and vertical lines. Only the three primary colours, red, blue and yellow, plus the non-colours white, grey and black.

The picture

Three blocks, black lines

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground. This page is laid out the same way.

Why it matters

Asymmetric balance

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

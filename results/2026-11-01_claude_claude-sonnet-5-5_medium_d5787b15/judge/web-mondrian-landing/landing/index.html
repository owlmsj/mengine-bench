This page is laid out the same way: a few colour blocks, thick black lines, a white ground.

De Stijl

The movement was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles are known as Neoplasticism:

  • only horizontal and vertical lines
  • the three primary colours: red, blue and yellow
  • the non-colours white, grey and black

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

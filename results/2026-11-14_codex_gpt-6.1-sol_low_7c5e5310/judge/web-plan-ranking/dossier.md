# Brazil's anti-inflation plans: editor's dossier

**Finding:** Real leads, followed by Collor I and Collor II. This is a ranking under the supplied rubric, not a causal verdict or a complete assessment of promises and social costs.

**Evidence and assumptions.** [Ipeadata](https://www.ipeadata.gov.br/ExibeSerie.aspx?module=M&serid=1410807112) attributes its annual IPCA series to IBGE/SNIPC and defines it as December against the previous December. The values match the [IBGE historical table indexed on the open web](https://www.ibge.gov.br/biblioteca/visualizacao/periodicos/236/inpc_ipca_2013_jul.pdf); direct retrieval of that PDF returned forbidden, so the accessible Ipeadata table is the operational source.

Launch dates below follow public launch/enactment. Real uses currency introduction rather than preparatory phases. Verão uses enactment rather than the following currency-change day; Collor I uses public announcement rather than the preceding legal instrument. “Whole calendar months” means completed monthly anniversaries, discarding the final partial month. The prescribed successor chain is followed exactly.

**Calculation and provenance.** Scores are our calculations from linked inputs using [RUBRIC.md](RUBRIC.md): score = 0.7 × clamp(1 − after/before, 0, 1) + 0.3 × min(months, 60)/60. All formula constants come from that rubric. Score links identify inflation inputs; months link to the starting-date source, with the successor's date linked in its own row. Real's [60-month duration](RUBRIC.md) is assigned, not observed. Exact scores and field-level URL provenance appear in [result.json](result.json).

**Interpretation.** Real combines the largest proportional reduction with maximum assigned durability. Collor I beats Collor II because its larger inflation reduction outweighs its shorter replacement interval. Cruzado, Bresser and Verão earn no reduction credit: comparison-year inflation exceeds their baseline. Their positive scores come entirely from time until replacement.

**Editorial caution.** Replacement intervals do not establish uninterrupted price stability. The comparison year can already contain a successor plan, so the measured outcome cannot be attributed exclusively to the named plan. Clamping treats all inflation increases alike. A podium place for a Collor plan therefore does not establish lasting stabilization. The [Central Bank account](https://www.bcb.gov.br/controleinflacao/planoreal) supports the broader interpretation of Real as the durable break with high inflation. The defensible headline is “Real leads the rubric”; avoid claiming that every podium plan fulfilled its promises.

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow. In 1930, Piet Mondrian brought primary colours and thick black lines into asymmetric balance.

The movement
De StijlNeoplasticism

A language of
lines and colour.

Horizontal and vertical lines. Red, blue and yellow. White, grey and black. These are the principles of Neoplasticism. Mondrian and Theo van Doesburg founded De Stijl in the Netherlands in 1917.

A few blocks.
A lasting reference.

The composition balances a large red square at the top right against a small blue square at the bottom left and a yellow rectangle at the bottom right. Its asymmetric balance became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

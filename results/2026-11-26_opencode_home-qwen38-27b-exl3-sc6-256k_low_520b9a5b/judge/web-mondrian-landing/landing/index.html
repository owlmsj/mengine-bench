The picture

De Stijl

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles — Neoplasticism — allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

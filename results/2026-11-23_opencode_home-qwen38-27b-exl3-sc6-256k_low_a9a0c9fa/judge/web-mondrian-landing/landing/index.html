The principles

Working within De Stijl, Mondrian pursued Neoplasticism: only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black. The picture places a large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

01 / WHY IT MATTERS

A balance that
reached beyond
the canvas.

A large red square. A small blue square. A small yellow rectangle. Thick black lines divide a white ground.

With just a few primary-colour blocks, Mondrian created an asymmetric balance that became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

The work belongs to De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, known as Neoplasticism, give this composition its visual language.

Horizontal. Vertical.

Only horizontal and vertical lines form the structure.

Three primary colours.

Red, blue and yellow give the composition its colour.

The non-colours.

White, grey and black complete the Neoplasticist palette.

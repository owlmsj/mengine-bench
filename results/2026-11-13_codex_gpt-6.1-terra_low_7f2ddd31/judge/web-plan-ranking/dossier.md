The launch dates are month precision, as published in the [Central Bank chronology](https://www.bcb.gov.br/conteudo/depec/NotasTecnicas/Nota_tecnica_49.pdf); month differences count whole calendar months between those months. Real is treated as beginning with the phased plan in February, rather than the later currency introduction. Real's duration is the rubric's fixed cap, not an observed end date. The chronology includes intervening plans, but intervals follow the specific successors named in the rubric. Annual rates are the [IBGE historical series](https://www.ibge.gov.br/biblioteca/visualizacao/periodicos/236/inpc_ipca_2012_jan.pdf).

Scores are calculated from the supplied rubric using unrounded inputs and displayed to six decimal places. Score links identify the inflation inputs; month links identify the chronology. The rubric supplies the weights, cap and successor mapping. The ranking is reproducible from those inputs and the companion JSON; it should not be presented as a causal evaluation.

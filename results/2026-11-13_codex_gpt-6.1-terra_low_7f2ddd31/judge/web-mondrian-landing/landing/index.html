A graphic interpretation of the composition

Balance, without symmetry.

Dutch painter Piet Mondrian made Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow in 1930. Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

The work belongs to De Stijl, a movement founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Mondrian. Its principles of Neoplasticism use horizontal and vertical lines, the primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

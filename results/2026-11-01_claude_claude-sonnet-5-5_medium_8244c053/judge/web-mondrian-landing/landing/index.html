Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Oil on canvas, Kunsthaus Zurich. Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

Painter
Piet Mondrian
Dutch, 1872-1944
Year
1930
Movement
De Stijl

A movement of straight lines

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles are known as Neoplasticism.

In this picture a large red square sits at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right. Thick black lines divide them on a white ground.

Neoplasticism, in three rules

  • Only horizontal and vertical lines.
  • The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.
  • The non-colours: white, grey and black.

Few colours

Three primaries and the non-colours. Nothing else.

Why it matters

The asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

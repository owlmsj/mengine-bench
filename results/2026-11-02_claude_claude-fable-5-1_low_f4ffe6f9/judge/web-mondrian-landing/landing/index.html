Three rules, nothing else

Mondrian's approach is called Neoplasticism. The painting is also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow. Every decision in it follows three restrictions.

1

Only horizontal and vertical lines.

2

Only the three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.

3

Only the non-colours white, grey and black.

What you see

A large red square at the top right. A small blue square at the bottom left. A small yellow rectangle at the bottom right. Thick black lines divide them on a white ground.

The diagram on this page is a schematic built from that description, not a reproduction of the canvas.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

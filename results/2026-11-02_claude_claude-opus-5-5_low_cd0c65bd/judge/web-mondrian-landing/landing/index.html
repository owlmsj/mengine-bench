Piet Mondrian · De Stijl

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

1930

The painting

Painted in 1930 by the Dutch painter Piet Mondrian (1872–1944), oil on canvas, now in the Kunsthaus Zurich. Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944), Dutch painter, co-founder of De Stijl.

The movement

De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

The rules

Neoplasticism: only horizontal and vertical lines; the primary colours red, blue and yellow; the non-colours white, grey and black.

What you see

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

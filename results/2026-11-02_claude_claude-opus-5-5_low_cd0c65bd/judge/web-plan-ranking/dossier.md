Assumptions: Real dated to the 1 Jul 1994 currency launch (URV phase began Feb 1994); months = whole months between launch dates; scores are computed, not sourced. Caveat: IPCA figures were taken from my knowledge of the IBGE series as reproduced on the cited pages and were not all re-fetched live within the time limit; editor should spot-check against IBGE SIDRA table 1737.

Reading: Cruzado, Bresser and Verao score ~0.07-0.10 because inflation the year after was *higher* than the year before. Collor I ranks 2nd mainly due to the 1989->1991 fall, which owes much to the 1990 asset freeze and recession, not lasting stabilisation.

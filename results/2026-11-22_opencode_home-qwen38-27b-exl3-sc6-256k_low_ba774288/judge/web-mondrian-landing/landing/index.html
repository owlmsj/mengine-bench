The movement

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. It pushed painting toward pure abstraction: no objects, no perspective, only structure.

4–5

The principles

Neoplasticism allowed only:

  • horizontal and vertical lines
  • the three primary colours — red, blue, yellow
  • the non-colours — white, grey, black
1930

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

The painter

Piet Mondrian

Dutch painter, 1872–1944. Co-founder of De Stijl, and the author of a canvas that needs only three colours and a set of straight black lines.

The work

Its title is Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow. You may also find it listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

The year 1930, oil on canvas

1917

De Stijl is founded in the Netherlands.

1930

The painting, fourteen years before his death in 1944.

The picture

Four decisions

Red

A large square at the top right.

Blue

A small square at the bottom left.

Yellow

A small rectangle at the bottom right.

Black on white

Thick lines divide everything, on a white ground.

The movement

De Stijl

Founded in the Netherlands, 1917 Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian

Its rules have a name: Neoplasticism. There are only three.

Only horizontal and vertical lines

No curves and no diagonals.

The three primary colours

Red, blue and yellow.

The non-colours

White, grey and black.

Graphic design
Architecture

Why it matters

A few blocks of primary colour, held in asymmetric balance.

That balance became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

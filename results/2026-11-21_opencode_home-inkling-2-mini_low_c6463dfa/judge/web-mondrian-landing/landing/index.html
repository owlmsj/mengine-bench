Oil on canvas · Kunsthaus Zurich

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Piet Mondrian's 1930 canvas: a large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

Why it matters

Mondrian painted in Neoplasticism: only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

The picture's asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

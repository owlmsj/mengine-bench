Piet Mondrian, 1872–1944 · Dutch painter

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Oil on canvas, 1930. Three primary-colour blocks, divided by thick black lines on a white ground, held in asymmetric balance.

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

01 The picture

Three blocks, one grid.

The title names the colours. The composition places them.

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

02 The movement

De Stijl,
1917.

Neoplasticism, as set out by the movement.

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Rule 01

Only horizontal and vertical lines.

Rule 02

The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.

Rule 03

The non-colours: white, grey and black.

03 Why it matters

A reference point.

What the painting left to what came after it.

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Piet Mondrian, 1930

1930

A large red square. A small blue square. A small yellow rectangle.

Divided by thick black lines on a white ground: the red at the top right, the blue at the bottom left, the yellow at the bottom right. Oil on canvas, painted in 1930, also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

The painter

Artist
Piet Mondrian (1872-1944), Dutch painter
Year
1930
Medium
Oil on canvas
Collection
Kunsthaus Zurich

The movement

De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

Neoplasticism: only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. He made Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow in 1930.

The movement

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Mondrian. Its Neoplastic principles use horizontal and vertical lines, primary colours, and white, grey and black.

The influence

The work’s asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

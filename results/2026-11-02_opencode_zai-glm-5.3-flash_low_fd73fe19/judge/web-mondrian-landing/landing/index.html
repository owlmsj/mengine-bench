The movement

De Stijl and Neoplasticism

De Stijl was founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its visual system, Neoplasticism, allowed only a handful of elements.

  • 01Only horizontal and vertical lines.
  • 02Only the three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.
  • 03Only the non-colours: white, grey and black.
The picture

What you see

A large red square

It sits at the top right of the canvas, the dominant block of colour.

Two smaller blocks

A small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right.

Thick black lines, white ground

The blocks are divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

Why it matters

Asymmetric balance, outsized influence

The painting balances a few primary-colour blocks asymmetrically: one large red field against small blue and yellow counterweights. That balance became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

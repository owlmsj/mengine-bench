Balance through asymmetry

A large red square occupies the upper right. A small blue square at the lower left and a small yellow rectangle at the lower right balance it across a white ground divided by thick black lines.

De Stijl and Neoplasticism

Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian founded De Stijl in the Netherlands in 1917.

Neoplasticism uses only horizontal and vertical lines, the primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.

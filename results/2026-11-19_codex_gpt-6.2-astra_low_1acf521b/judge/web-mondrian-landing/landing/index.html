De Stijl

Dutch painter Piet Mondrian and Theo van Doesburg founded De Stijl in the Netherlands in 1917.

Neoplasticism used only horizontal and vertical lines, the primary colours red, blue and yellow, and the non-colours white, grey and black.

Why it matters

The asymmetric balance of just a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

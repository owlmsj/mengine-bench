The painter

Piet Mondrian, 1872–1944

Piet Mondrian was a Dutch painter. In 1930 he made Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, an oil on canvas that is also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow. The painting is held in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

The movement

De Stijl

The work belongs to De Stijl, a movement founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, known as Neoplasticism, allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours — red, blue and yellow — and the non-colours white, grey and black.

The picture

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance

The painting's asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

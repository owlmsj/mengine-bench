# Dossier: Which of Brazil's anti-inflation plans delivered?

**For the editor.** Ranking of six stabilization plans (1986–1994) scored by a fixed rubric: 70% on how much the annual IPCA (IBGE, calendar-year %) fell between the year before and the year after launch, 30% on how long the plan lasted before its successor (capped at 60 months). Score = 0.7·drop + 0.3·durability, on a 0–1 scale.

## The ranking

| Rank | Plan | Launched | IPCA before (Y−1) | IPCA after (Y+1) | Months lasted | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real | 1994 | 2477.15% | 22.41% | 60 (cap) | **0.9937** |
| 2 | Collor I | 1990 | 1972.91% | 472.70% | 10 | **0.5823** |
| 3 | Collor II | 1991 | 1620.97% | 1119.10% | 42 | **0.4267** |

| 6 | Verao | 1989 | 980.21% | 1620.97% | 14 | 0.0700 |

## Top 3, best first

1. **Plano Real (1994)** — the only plan that actually killed inflation: annual IPCA collapsed from 2477.15% (1993) to 22.41% (1995), a 99.1% drop, and it is still the regime in force, so it takes the full 60-month durability credit. Score 0.9937.
2. **Plano Collor I (1990)** — cut annual IPCA from 1972.91% (1989) to 472.70% (1991), a 76% drop, but lasted only 10 months before its own successor, so durability is low. Score 0.5823.
3. **Plano Collor II (1991)** — trimmed IPCA from 1620.97% (1990) to 1119.10% (1992), a 31% drop, and held 42 months. Score 0.4267.

The 1980s plans (Cruzado, Bresser, Verao) all score near zero: each was followed by a year with *higher* inflation than the year before it, so the drop term clamps to 0 and only a small durability credit remains.

## Assumptions (where the brief left room)

- **Launch dates.** Cruzado 28 Feb 1986; Bresser 12 Jun 1987; Verao 15 Jan 1989; Collor I 16 Mar 1990; Collor II 31 Jan 1991; Real 1 Jul 1994 (currency circulation; the URV anchor was created 27 Feb 1994). I used the circulation date for Real since that is when the plan took effect.
- **IPCA** = IBGE annual (calendar-year) variation, in percent. Values 1985–1995 from the IBGE consolidated series via Ipeadata.
- **months_lasted** = whole calendar months between one plan's launch and the next plan's launch (Real has no successor, so the rubric's 60-month cap applies).
- **ipca_before / ipca_after** use the calendar years Y−1 and Y+1 of the launch year, per the rubric.

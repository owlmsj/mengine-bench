Piet Mondrian, 1930

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow. A few blocks of primary colour, held in place by thick black lines on a white ground.

Painter
Piet Mondrian

Dutch painter, 1872-1944

Year
1930

Oil on canvas

Movement
De Stijl

Founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian

Collection
Kunsthaus Zurich

The rules: Neoplasticism

The picture keeps strictly to De Stijl's principles:

  • only horizontal and vertical lines;
  • the three primary colours, red, blue and yellow;
  • the non-colours white, grey and black.

The picture

A large red square at the top right, a small blue square at the bottom left and a small yellow rectangle at the bottom right, divided by thick black lines on a white ground.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

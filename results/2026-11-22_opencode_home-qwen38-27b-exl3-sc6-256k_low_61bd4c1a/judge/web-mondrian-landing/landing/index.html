Piet Mondrian (1872–1944), Dutch painter

Also listed as “Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow.”

The painter

Piet Mondrian, 1872–1944. A Dutch painter who helped found De Stijl.

The movement

De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian.

The picture

Primary-colour blocks in asymmetric balance, separated by black lines on white.

Why it matters

Its asymmetric balance of a few primary-colour blocks became a reference for modern graphic design and architecture.

Neoplasticism: the rules behind the picture

01

Only horizontal and vertical lines.

02

The three primary colours: red, blue and yellow.

03

The non-colours: white, grey and black.

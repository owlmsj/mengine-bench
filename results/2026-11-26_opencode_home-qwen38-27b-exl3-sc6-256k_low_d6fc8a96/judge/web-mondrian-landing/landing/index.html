The painter

Piet Mondrian (1872–1944) was a Dutch painter. In 1930 he made Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, also listed as Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow, in oil on canvas. It is held in the collection of the Kunsthaus Zurich.

The movement

The work belongs to De Stijl, founded in the Netherlands in 1917 by Theo van Doesburg and Piet Mondrian. Its principles, called Neoplasticism, allow only horizontal and vertical lines, the three primary colours — red, blue and yellow — and the non-colours white, grey and black.
